List every file in the research directory, with its size.

Your only way to see what is on disk: there is no `ls`, `glob` or
`grep`. This says what exists; `index.md`, when there is one, says
what each page holds.

Call it when you need to know the state of the work rather than out of
habit — at the start of a run, to find out whether there is a wiki to
continue; before naming a new page, since another researcher may have
taken the name; and after a long stretch of work, when pages written
earlier have fallen out of your context but not off the disk.

Files you did not write are earlier research this run was pointed at.
Read them before repeating their searches, extend them rather than
starting over, and cite them as what they are.

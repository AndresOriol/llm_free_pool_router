# Research Request: Market & Competitor Analysis for Computer Vision Hardware + Software Startup in Spain

## Date: 2026-09-10

## Context & Team
- Founders: 2 young entrepreneurs based in Spain.
- Proposition: Commercializing adaptable hardware with integrated software (Edge AI appliance) to apply Computer Vision (CV) with an implementation/integration phase.
- Target Sectors:
  1. Retail Security: Detecting theft / suspicious behavior in retail stores.
  2. Industrial Quality Control: Defect detection and inline inspection, integrating external sensors (sensor fusion) to provide predictive quality insights.

## Core Research Objectives & Workstreams
1. **Workstream 1: Retail Security & Theft Detection (Spain / EU)**
   - Direct competitors and substitutes (e.g., Veesion, Vaidio, BriefCam, Milestone, Axis, local Spanish integrators like Securitas/Prosegur partnerships, Dahua/Hikvision AI NVRs).
   - Technology and business model: Hardware appliance vs SaaS vs cloud, pricing structures (SaaS per camera/month vs upfront hardware + license).
   - Regulatory and legal barriers: Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) rulings on biometrics/behavioral analysis, GDPR constraints on public/retail CCTV, and EU AI Act classification (biometrics, emotion recognition, real-time surveillance restrictions).
   - Buyer persona and sales cycle: Supermarkets (Mercadona, Dia, Carrefour), franchise retail, pharmacy, small shops. Margins and willingness to pay.

2. **Workstream 2: Industrial Quality Control & Sensor Integration (Spain / EU)**
   - Direct competitors, machine vision vendors, and systems integrators in Spain (e.g., Cognex, Keyence, Basler, SICK, Neural Labs, Bcnvision, Ibermática, AIS Vision, Kivnon, Plain Concepts).
   - Technology & Integration requirements: Custom optics, lighting, PLC/SCADA integration, edge compute (Nvidia Jetson, industrial PCs), sensor fusion (thermal, vibration, acoustic).
   - Business model & unit economics: Turnkey project fees, recurring software maintenance, hardware margins, typical implementation timelines.
   - Buyer persona and barriers: Automotive (SEAT/Stellantis tiers), food & beverage, packaging, pharma. High liability, proof-of-concept fatigue, long sales cycles.

3. **Synthesis & Comparative Strategic Recommendation**
   - Head-to-head comparison of Retail Security vs. Industrial Quality Control for a 2-person founding team with limited capital in Spain.
   - Resource requirements, technical feasibility, legal risk, sales velocity, defensibility, and recommended beachhead.

# Explore research evidence review

*Branch `codex/improve-explore-research`; 2026-09-10; audit of the completed Machintl control and candidate research reports against source text retained in their traces.*

The reports have useful structure, but neither is reliable enough to support the founders' sector choice without targeted source repair. The control contains one arithmetic/denominator error and an unsupported ROI conclusion. The candidate improves presentation but has a citation-scope failure: its industrial claims cite retail sources, and its legal conclusions use a vendor's legal-marketing page rather than an authority. These are evidence failures, not a verdict on the Claude reference session, which is only the intended research behaviour.

## 1. Scope and method

This review sampled decision-critical claims only: Spanish retail-loss economics, payback, privacy risk, sector comparison, and report provenance. It used the retrieved text embedded in [control trace](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/trace.json) and [candidate trace](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/trace.json), not new web research. “Unsupported” means the cited/retrieved material does not establish the conclusion; “contradicted” means the trace supplies contrary facts.

The related [capability issue queue](2026-09-10-explore-capability-issues.md) already covers search-budget, incomplete-workstream, and broad evidence-quality hypotheses. This artifact adds the concrete report/source cases that can become fixed evaluation fixtures.

## 2. Control: retail loss and ROI are not decision-safe

1. **Contradicted denominator — €1.856bn is relabelled as theft.** The report correctly says that €1.856bn is *pérdida desconocida* at [control report line 27](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/workdir/research/final_report.md), then says external shoplifting is 57% of that measure at [line 28](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/workdir/research/final_report.md). The retained Diario de Transporte/AECOC result in control trace turn 22, lines 116 and 124, likewise defines the €1.856bn as unknown loss and apportions 57% to external theft, 23% administrative error, 15% internal theft and 4% supplier fraud. The comparison nevertheless calls it “€1.856B annual theft loss” ([line 117](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/workdir/research/final_report.md)). That latter claim is contradicted by the cited source; it overstates the theft-addressable denominator.

2. **Unsupported payoff claim.** “A system that reduces shrinkage by 40% to 60% pays for itself within months” appears at [control report line 54](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/workdir/research/final_report.md). Its three citations are Veesion, Everseen and the AECOC news item (source list [lines 151–157](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/workdir/research/final_report.md)); none records a system price, deployment/operating cost, store-level baseline, attributable reduction, or calculation. The source text in trace turn 22 supplies aggregate loss and composition only. This is unsupported, rather than factually disproved.

3. **Unsupported blanket privacy conclusion.** The comparison says industrial work has “No personal data processed” ([control report line 121](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/workdir/research/final_report.md)) and the conclusion calls it “zero privacy risks” ([line 143](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/workdir/research/final_report.md)). The research request itself only specifies CV, sensors and factories ([control trace turn 43, request lines 4–11](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_control/trace.json)); it does not establish that employees, visitors, identifiers, or remotely administered devices are absent. The cited integration material does not close that gap. This is unsupported, not disproved: a defined deployment that excludes people may have a different outcome.

## 3. Candidate: source identity and citation scope break the comparison

1. **Legal conclusions come from the vendor whose product is being assessed.** Candidate report claims that the CNIL and AEPD “rule” on behavioural analysis, legitimate interest and DPIAs ([lines 53–56](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)). Its only listed authority for those numbered citations is “[7] CNIL CCTV & GDPR Legality (Veesion Legal Analysis)” ([line 150](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)); candidate trace turn 15 retrieved that `veesion.io` page. The underlying source is a supplier's legal explanation, not a CNIL/AEPD decision or guidance. That does not disprove every legal statement, but it leaves them unsupported at the required authority level and misdescribes the evidence as a regulator ruling.

2. **Industrial citations point to the retail bibliography.** The industrial comparison uses [3], [5] and [6] for industrial sales cycles and validation ([candidate report line 106](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)), and [1], [4] and [6] for its technical-complexity assessment ([line 107](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)). The bibliography maps those numbers to Spot AI/Veesion, Business Insider on Veesion, and Veesion case studies ([lines 144–150](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)), while industrial sources begin only at [8] ([line 151](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)). Candidate trace turns 12–15 show the retail retrievals. These industrial claims are unsupported by their cited sources; this is a mechanical citation-binding failure, not a finding that the claims are false.

3. **Report date is stale.** The candidate declares “Date of Analysis: June 2026” ([candidate report line 23](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)), but its trace run starts `2026-09-10T09:54:11Z` ([candidate trace `run.start`](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/trace.json)). The report also cites pages dated July and September 2026 in the trace (turns 12–13). This is factually disproved provenance, albeit not a market conclusion.

4. **“Low” industrial regulatory risk remains unsupported.** Candidate comparison says industrial work processes no personal data ([candidate report line 105](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)); the next-action design assumes cameras and sensors but never asks whether workers enter view ([lines 125–128](../../evals/results/runs/20260910_explore_machintl_candidate/workdir/research/final_report.md)). The retrieved industrial material includes no deployment-specific exclusion. Treat it as a question for discovery, not a sector-wide factual conclusion.

## 4. Proposed future evaluation contracts

1. **Loss denominator fixture.** Provide the AECOC excerpt used in control trace turn 22. A passing report must label €1.856bn as unknown loss, preserve its composition, and must not call the whole amount theft.
2. **ROI calculation gate.** Any payback recommendation must cite or declare the five inputs: customer baseline, addressable share, attributable reduction, one-off cost, and recurring cost. Absence of an input requires an explicit unknown, not “months.”
3. **Authority-class legal fixture.** Supply a vendor compliance page plus an official regulator excerpt. Regulatory conclusions must cite the official material; vendor pages may describe product claims only.
4. **Citation-scope checker.** Give separate retail and industrial source sets with numbered references. Every industrial factual sentence must resolve to an industrial source, or be labelled an assumption/recommendation.
5. **Deployment-privacy challenge.** Present industrial cameras with ambiguous worker visibility. A passing answer asks the decisive scoping questions and conditions the GDPR conclusion; it cannot return “no” or “zero” risk without facts.

## 5. Use in the harness

These contracts should first be recorded as probes or fixed-source scenarios and linked to their trace evidence, then evaluated under the promotion rule in [the evaluation method](../../docs/08-evaluation-method.md). They test research conduct and claim support, not whether the Claude reference's recommendation is correct.
